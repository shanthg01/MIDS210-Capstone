The Idea

Quantitative evaluation beats manual scouting

Every D1 season produces thousands of transfer portal entries. Most programs still evaluate them the same way they always have — box scores, tape, word of mouth. PortalPoint's thesis is simple: a program that scores every eligible player against its own system and roster, at scale, finds better fits faster than a program that can't.

What It Enables

One fit score, four real signals behind it

Every transfer-eligible player, scored against every program, refreshed as the portal moves.

◎

System Fit

Cosine similarity between a player's shot profile and play-type tendencies and a program's own offensive system.

▦

Roster Fit

How well a player's statistical profile fills the specific archetype and positional holes on your current roster.

◷

Role Fit

Projected minutes, usage, and rotation slot if this player joined your program, with uncertainty intervals.

↗

Player Projection

Context-neutral and destination-adjusted talent forecasts, built on a shrinkage + state-space model over multi-season play.

Σ

Team Impact

Projected effect on your program's overall efficiency margin if the player joins, from a roster-counterfactual model.

✓

Transfer Success

Probability a given transfer actually outperforms its projection at the destination, calibrated on real historical transfers.

Data Sources

  • BartTorvik — ratings, four factors
  • Hoop Explorer — RAPM, play types
  • hoopR / ESPN play-by-play
  • 247Sports — transfer portal events

Modeling

  • K-Means player & team clustering
  • Cosine-similarity fit scoring
  • Gradient-boosted playing-time model
  • State-space player projection (Kalman)
  • Empirical-Bayes transfer-success model

Platform

  • FastAPI · PostgreSQL + pgvector · Redis
  • React + TypeScript frontend
  • MLflow model tracking, S3 artifacts
